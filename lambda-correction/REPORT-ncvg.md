# Lambda correction for curve-blocked NCV, Poisson and binomial: report

Derivation `NOTE-ncvg.qmd`; code `ncvg-lib.R`, `run-lc-ncvg.R`; tables `summaries-ncvg/`. Generated by
`make-report-ncvg.R`. 12 core cells (Poisson, binomial; iid, OU, FPC errors; G = 40, 100; mid signal) x
200 replicates, same data as the study. CL2 as in the study (on Vp). +J H^-1 J' = mgcv-style term with
the exact NCV Hessian (not a calibrated variance). IJ = Var(rho-hat) from the NCV curve scores; × = with
the cross term. fixλ = CL2 of a different estimator: the refit at the cell's geometric-mean NCV lambda,
computed from the same replicates (a hindsight reference, not an oracle; it breaks down where lambda-hat
is often on a boundary, see below); its SE/SD divides by its own Monte Carlo SD. Nominal level 95%;
z = normal critical value (t_{G-1} rows are in the CSV). Coverage and SE/SD on the link scale. Primary
criteria: coverage and interval score; the SE/SD ratios are descriptive (neither is a calibration
diagnostic for an SE whose inflation is heavy-tailed over replicates).

Checks: CL2 coverage vs the study's published NCV coverage, 48 cell x estimand rows, max |difference| 4.4e-16. NCV estimates vs the study's stored ones: 0 of 2400 replicates differ by more than 1e-6 relative (max absolute 0.0e+00). Failed tasks: 0. NCV fits fully converged: 2400 of 2400. Time per task: median 24 s, total 14.4 core-hours. MC SEs of paired differences treat cells as independent.

- fixλ in the binomial G = 40 dependent cells: coverage 70.4 at median SE/SD 1.11 against its own SD and width x0.70: the geometric mean of a lambda-hat distribution with 21.8% of fits at a boundary (per cell and penalty, up to 35.0%) oversmooths, so its undercoverage is bias. It is no benchmark there.
- Var(rho-hat): median IJ SD / MC SD of log lambda-hat 0.35-1.35 (Poisson) and 0.15-1.00 (binomial; MC SD 0.9-6.3 on the log scale): for binomial, lambda-hat jumps between interior and boundary solutions, which a first-order term cannot represent; boundary components get zero variance by construction.
- Targeting (dependent cells, Spearman): IJ 0.17 to 0.46, mgcv-style term 0.23 to 0.51: the gain comes from the size of the term, not from better targeting.
- Independent errors: CL2 already overcovers beta (98.3 / 97.7); IJ costs interval score x1.029 / x1.048 (Poisson / binomial).
