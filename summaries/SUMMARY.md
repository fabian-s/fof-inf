# Result tables

Aggregate results of the main studies, read by the paper, the supplement and `../writeup/RESULTS.qmd`.

- `final/`: synthetic study (`analysis/run-analysis.R` and the other analysis scripts; `REPORT.md` from
  `analysis/write-report.R`).
- `plasmode/`: plasmode study (`plasmode/analyse-plasmode.R`, `plasmode/diagnostics.R`).
- `hybrid/`: NCV-centred hybrid intervals and coverage maps (`analysis/hybrid-intervals.R`).
- `dti/`, `dti-case/`: DTI application (`analysis/dti-application.R`, `analysis/dti-application-case.R`).
